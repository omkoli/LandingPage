Forge Report · Session Verdict

BetaCheck

A bug-bounty-shaped marketplace for pre-launch MVPs, tested to destruction.

Idea: AI-app usability testing marketplace  ·  Goal: harden, critique, validate  ·  Session: 2026-07-03
HARDENED
Survived four rounds of attack. Mechanism concrete. Cracks that held are implementation-tractable, not existential.

What was locked

  • Customer — solo founders with a shipped-but-unlaunched MVP, largely vibe-coded via Lovable, v0, Bolt, or Cursor. Budget-sensitive.
  • Wedge — INR-paid India tester supply against USD-billed founder revenue; time-zone offset delivers overnight turnaround as a feature.
  • Scope — functional bugs, spec-conformance failures, task-blockers, data-integrity, first-run failures. Explicitly not security.
  • Payment mechanism — hybrid: ~$8 base per completed report + severity-weighted bonuses ($3 minor / $10 major / $25 critical), $60 bonus cap per report, founders buy in packs.
  • Adjudication — every finding in Categories B/C/E must cite a specific line of the founder's guidelines document. Triage becomes matching, not judgment.
  • Programs — public + private toggle, private deferred to v1.1 behind a tester ranking system.
  • Cold start — hybrid Plan A (manual concierge on tester supply, ~$960 subsidy + heavy founder time) + Plan C (AI-tool platform distribution partnership on founder demand). Friends as demo cohort only.
  • AI role — filter for spam and duplicates, first-pass extraction. Never adjudicator.

What was killed — and why

  • "AI-generated app" as a market segment — justified only by trend timing. Trend is not a moat; testing needs don't fundamentally differ from any MVP.
  • Pure per-bug bounty at MVP-founder budgets — economics don't math; tester cold-start impossible without base pay.
  • Pure per-report Maze-clone model — collapses differentiation. Maze wins a commodity race on scale.
  • Synack-shaped enterprise pivot — customer mismatch. $50K/engagement contracts vs. $500 MVP-founder budgets.
  • "AI + HITL validation" as adjudicator — hand-wave without a defined AI job. Retained only as a filter.
  • Freeform "business logic flaws" scope — straddled Hackerone territory. Narrowed to spec-conformance failures only.

Cracks that held — parked, not resolved

  • Pre-launch trust & IP paranoia — resolvable with NDA click-through, screenshot redaction, kill-switch access revoke. Wizard step 8, v1.
  • Wizard funnel completion rate — must exceed ~50% or business fails; save/resume and AI-drafted aha moment are required from day one.
  • Auto-extraction hallucination — mitigated by source snippets + one-click confirm per claim.
  • Guidelines drift — 7-day passive re-crawl; active per-report crawl in v1.1.
  • Tester ranking / reputation system — needed before private programs unlock.
  • Cross-border payment ops — Payoneer, Wise, TDS, W-8BEN, entity structure. Legal + finance workstream.

What has to be true before any code

Five vibe-coded MVP founders sit down with the Guidelines Wizard. Three or more complete it end-to-end within ten minutes and produce guidelines a tester could actually test against. If that fails, the model needs a rethink — not another design cycle.

Friends are your demo cohort, not your economics. Real supply-side proof starts at week six, after loyalty burns off.
